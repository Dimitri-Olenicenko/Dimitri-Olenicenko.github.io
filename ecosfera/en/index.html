Something to think about before the start
The first move of a leader shaping the company's AI strategy is clarity: which capabilities exactly you want to build, and why. Three questions worth answering before anything is built:
  1. 01What should it be able to do?

    Which AI capabilities you need — see everything every morning, catch deviations, free the managers' hands — and why those in particular.

  2. 02What return?

    Restaurant revenue growth, hotel cost savings or new capability in the team — and how you will measure it in six months.

  3. 03Who leads and who uses it?

    How you will state this goal to the team, who owns the result, and what the first action this week is.
