Phase 2

Telegram bot: notifications & reporting on one database

US$ 2,000fixed
about 3 weeks
after Phase 1 is accepted

The process leaves WhatsApp. Every checkpoint sends a notification, every number lands in one database, and management gets the report without asking.

You get

  • A Telegram bot: staff act in a private chat, management receives the reports in a group, complaints and maintenance have their own topics.
  • Notifications at the checkpoints agreed in Phase 1 — data not entered by the deadline, a task overdue, a complaint open longer than 24 hours, a deviation from normal — with escalation to the responsible person.
  • Automatic reporting: the daily morning report and the weekly summary, sent on schedule; on-demand reports by command.
  • One database that stores every entry, task, notification and report — history from day one, exportable to Sheets.
  • Hosting under the company's own account (≈ US$ 6 per month), documentation, and training for the person who will run it.

We need from you

  • Staff on Telegram (or the decision to move them) and the list of departments and responsible people.
  • The thresholds for "normal" — or we start the alerts silently and you switch each one on when it proves right.
  • An account for hosting (we set it up together; it stays yours).
Done when the bot has run for one full week on live data with the reports arriving on schedule and the notifications reaching the right people.

How we work

Small, owned, and yours to run

Terms

  • Fixed price per phase, invoiced per phase. No subscription to us.
  • A phase starts on your written go-ahead and ends with the acceptance written next to it above.
  • Everything built — code, database, bot, hosting account — is registered to the company. If we part ways, nothing stops working.
  • Documentation and hands-on training are part of every phase: the system belongs to the company, not to the vendor.
  • Running costs after handover ≈ US$ 6 per month for hosting; Telegram, Cloudbeds API and Google Sheets carry no extra fee.

What we do not do

  • We do not replace Cloudbeds, Olsera or your accounting — we read from them and connect them.
  • We do not let an AI touch the numbers or send anything on its own; it only drafts, a person confirms.
  • We do not build a big system first and ask questions later — Phase 1 exists so that Phases 2 and 3 are built on facts.
  • We do not lock you in: Phase 2 stands on its own, and Phase 3 is optional until you have seen Phase 2 run.
