    07How to read this report
    • Income is gross sales as recorded in each monthly sheet (food, non-alcoholic drinks, alcohol).
    • Expenses in this file are cost of goods only: consumption = opening stock + purchases + transfers in − transfers out − staff meals − closing stock. The file has no payroll, rent, utilities or marketing, so "margin after cost of sales" is not profit.
    • Cost of sales % = consumption ÷ gross sales, the same ratio the source sheets use.
    • The "SC" column (about 9.1% of gross sales every month, consistent with a 10% service charge included in prices) is not deducted. All figures are gross sales.
    • "Typical" ranges are common rules of thumb for full-service restaurants (food 25–32%, alcohol 18–24%, non-alcoholic drinks 15–25%), not figures from this file.
